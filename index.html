EDUCATOR SUPPORT

Teacher Guide & Local Progress View

Use SplicEd before or alongside supervised laboratory work. This prototype can summarize learner profiles saved on this browser; cross-device real-time monitoring requires an authenticated database/backend.

01

Recommended lesson flow

Orient learners to safety and objectives → Learn/Reference → Guided Mode → Guided Order → Independent Challenge → Assessment → hands-on laboratory performance with instructor supervision.

02

Differentiation

Use Guided Mode for learners who need prompts, step labels, review, and feedback. Use Independent Mode when learners are ready for reduced cues and procedural recall.

03

How to interpret progress

Progress indicators show exposure, practice, sequencing attempts, and assessment results. They do not by themselves prove practical electrical competence or long-term mastery.

04

Practical boundary

Verify conductor preparation, turns, finishing, tool handling, and safety using the instructor-approved procedure and actual laboratory performance.

CURRICULUM BASELINE ALIGNMENT

How SplicEd positions its content

CHED / TUP

Defines the higher-education and Industrial Arts program context of the intended users and local course implementation.

DepEd EIM

Provides a baseline instructional curriculum reference for Electrical Installation and Maintenance concepts, terminology, and progression where applicable.

TESDA EIM NC II

Provides technical competency and performance-standard reference for electrical wiring termination/connection and splice-related competencies.

Exact classroom procedures, strip lengths, turns, materials, and safety requirements follow the validated local instructional specification.

COMPETENCY-ALIGNED CONTENT

What the module content is anchored to

01

Higher-education context: CHED + TUP

SplicEd is designed for TUP Manila Industrial Arts students. CHED provides the higher-education program context, while the local course and instructor-validated procedure provide the immediate classroom basis.

02

Baseline instructional alignment: DepEd EIM

DepEd Electrical Installation and Maintenance curriculum materials are used as a baseline instructional reference for relevant concepts, terminology, and progression where the same electrical competencies are addressed.

03

Technical competency reference: TESDA EIM NC II

TESDA Electrical Installation and Maintenance NC II provides technical competency and performance-standard reference for terminating/connecting electrical wiring and splice-related competencies.

04

Practical boundary

SplicEd prepares learners cognitively. Actual competency still requires supervised hands-on performance, safety checks, and instructor assessment.

THEORY-ALIGNED DESIGN

Why SplicEd is designed this way

01

Cognitive Load

Procedures are divided into manageable segments to avoid overwhelming the learner.

02

Multimedia Learning

Visual demonstrations are paired with concise explanations.

03

Scaffolding

Learners move from guided demonstration toward independent practice.

04

Mastery Learning

Learners can review procedures, retry activities, and improve before moving forward.
