THE PEOPLE BEHIND SPLICED

About Us

Learning procedures, one step at a time.

SplicEd is a gamified web-based learning system developed by Group 5, BSIE-ICT 4A, at the Technological University of the Philippines — Manila.

It supports procedural understanding of electrical splices and joints through lessons, visual demonstrations, sequencing practice, feedback, and module assessments.
